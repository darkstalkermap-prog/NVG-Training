Short answer

29. List three things the driver and ground guide agree on in the brief before moving.
Any three: signals; type of light (filtered visible or IR); which side the guide works from; that the route is walked first; what to do if a light fails or sight is lost (driver stops).

30. Explain why following distance and closing speed are hard to judge on the PVS-14, and what you do about it.
Monocular = poor depth perception (one eye, no binocular cues). Increase interval per convoy brief, use IR markers, reduce speed, never tailgate.

Scoring suggestion: 1 point each for 1–28, 3 points each for 29–30 (total 34). Set the pass mark per unit SOP.
